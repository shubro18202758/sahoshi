One live picture of Kolkata's roads, rain and crowds for the traffic police, KMC, disaster response and ambulance (108) crews. On-premises open-source AI proposes; a named officer decides.

29 October 2026 · github.com/shubro18202758/sahoshi · MIT licence

1 · What exists today

  • A working system on one on-premises AI computer — the live stack reports READY 12/12: road store, live traffic ingest, decision engine, operator console (Bengali, English, Hindi), event emulator, OSINT ingest, three local language/embedding model hosts.
  • Five AI services running beside it: corridor forecasting (Chronos-2), waterlogging nowcast (rain × Hooghly tide × terrain × pumps), a signal-timing traffic twin, Bengali voice (speech-to-text and read-aloud) and camera road state (Qwen3-VL).
  • Real Kolkata roads: 49 junctions and 65 road segments (125 km), the Nabanna → airport route 29 km from OpenStreetMap; a 163-place gazetteer in English, Bengali and Hindi (wards, police stations, hospitals, pandals, ghats, low-lying spots).
  • Open feeds wired in: IMD / NDMA SACHET alerts for West Bengal, Bengali and English Kolkata news, KMC notices and Kolkata Traffic Police advisories, Open-Meteo rainfall and air, NASA FIRMS fire hotspots, GDACS cyclones, CWC river gauges, USGS quakes, the Hooghly tide (modelled), Copernicus 30 m terrain, KMC FFEWS adapter (emulated).
  • Quality: 68 automated gates green: 6,212 Python tests, 121 Rust tests, 45 console unit suites, contract replays and type checks.

Labels used throughout: LIVE (a real open feed), OBSERVED (a measured record), MODELLED (computed under stated assumptions), SIMULATED (an exercise scenario), EMULATED (a stand-in for a feed that needs an agency's access).

9 · Scalability — a city is a folder

Everything city-specific lives in a City Pack (cities/<id>/): the OpenStreetMap extract and corridors, gazetteer, agencies, emergency numbers, plate format, languages, scenarios and impact parameters. The prototype was first built for another Indian city; the same build tools now produce Kolkata, and a test builds both. Next packs: Howrah and Bidhannagar (inside the Kolkata pack), Siliguri, Asansol–Durgapur, Patna and Ranchi (Hindi is already first-class).

10 · Guardrails

  • Vehicles only — no faces, no persons; number plates masked until a named duty officer is on duty.
  • The AI proposes; a named officer approves every action; no answer — the current plan holds; every decision audited.
  • Honest labels on every number and every feed not yet connected.
  • Events, not people: news and social signals are aggregate and about places; posters are never stored or scored.
  • No e-challan can be approved against a theft victim.
  • On-premises, retention limits, in the spirit of the DPDP Act 2023.
