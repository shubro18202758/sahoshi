## 9. Open questions on rights

These need a decision by the team before the material is used beyond evaluation. Nothing here has been removed.

1. **The State Emblem of India** is part of six marks (`kp.png`, `wbp.svg`, `bpc.png`, `hcp.png`, `wbsdma.png`,
   `imd.png`). Its use is restricted by the State Emblem of India (Prohibition of Improper Use) Act, 2005. Showing
   it in a private prototype can suggest an official link that does not exist. Two screenshots show the Kolkata
   Police badge (`map-forecast.jpg`, `advisories.jpg`). Options: written permission from each agency, or Sahoshi's
   own generic agency badges (`services/command-deck/static/illustrations/agencies/`) in place of these marks.
2. **Government of West Bengal marks**: the Biswa Bangla mark inside `wbtc.png`, the Government of West Bengal seal
   in `wbsdma.png`, and the KMC emblem `kmc.png` (also in `advisories.jpg`). Same options as above.
3. **Logos without a licence for this use**: five are "non-free, fair use" on en.wikipedia (`wbp`, `bpc`, `hcp`,
   `ndma`, `imd`), a rationale that covers Wikipedia, not this repository; six state no licence (`nkda`,
   `wbsdma`, `ndma-sachet`, `wbtc`, `open-meteo`, `cesium`); the Copernicus logo's Commons licence is doubtful.
4. **Cesium ion's free plan** shows "Upgrade for commercial use" in several screenshots. A government pilot should
   confirm its ion plan, or use Google's Map Tiles API and Microsoft's imagery terms directly. Some screenshots cut
   the imagery credits (`map-3d.jpg`, and the decide and home shots where a panel covers the map's corner).
5. **The fallback voice's lineage.** Svara-TTS v1 descends, through Canopy Labs' Orpheus 3B, from Meta's
   Llama 3.2 3B, so the Llama 3.2 Community License may also apply to it (its attribution and acceptable-use
   terms). Indic Parler-TTS is the live voice; Svara answers only when Parler cannot load.
6. **Open-Meteo's free API** is for non-commercial use. A city deployment needs Open-Meteo's commercial API or a
   self-hosted instance (Open-Meteo's server is open source).
7. **Low.** The Durga Puja pandal and Kumartuli idol photographs show temporary artworks; India's
   freedom-of-panorama exception covers works permanently in a public place. The KMC and Kolkata Traffic Police
   fixtures hold link anchors copied from those sites; the CWC fixture holds recorded river levels with no stated
   licence.
