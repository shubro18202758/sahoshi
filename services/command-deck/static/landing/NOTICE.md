# static/landing — the landing page's console screenshots

The landing page (`/welcome`) shows **no console screenshot for now**. The screenshots it used to carry were taken on
the platform's previous city network; they were withdrawn when Sahoshi moved to Kolkata, because a picture of another
city's roads must never stand for Kolkata's.

New screenshots are taken once the Kolkata road network and scenarios are live in the stack, the same way as before:

- a development deck of this tree, backed by a sandbox copy of the stack running **exercise (emulated) data**, every
  feed labelled so on screen;
- No duty officer was named, so every registration mark stays **masked** and "Plates hidden" shows in the top bar;
- images resized and encoded to WebP only (`console-<screen>-{800,1600}.webp`), nothing else edited;
- captioned on the page "Console screenshot · exercise data", with third-party content credited on screen.

Until then the page shows Kolkata reference photographs (static/photos, each labelled "Reference photo · not live"
with its credit) and Sahoshi's own illustrations (static/artwork).
