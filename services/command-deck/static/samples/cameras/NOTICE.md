# Sample camera stills: credits and licences

No live CCTV frame reaches Sahoshi, so a camera page offers these **sample stills** to read with the road-state model.
Every one is shown labelled "Sample still · not live" with its credit beside it.

They are copies of the vision service's test images (`services/vision/tests/fixtures`, see its `NOTICE.md`): 1024 px
renditions of files on Wikimedia Commons, with every visible face and legible number plate **pixelated by us**
(`services/vision/tests/fixtures/prepare_fixtures.py`); otherwise unchanged. No face is the subject of any image. The
modified versions are shared under the same licences as the originals (CC BY-SA files stay CC BY-SA). Marks or text
visible in the photos belong to their owners.
